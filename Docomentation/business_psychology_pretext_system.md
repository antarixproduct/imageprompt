
# Business + Post Type Psychology Pretext System

## Structure

```text
BUSINESS TYPE
→ POST TYPE
→ CUSTOMER EMOTION
→ INTERNAL THOUGHT
→ VISUAL PSYCHOLOGY
→ DECISION TRIGGER
```

---

# 1. COMPUTER INSTITUTE

## Admission Open
The design should instantly make students and parents feel:
“This is the right place to build my future.”

Show the emotional struggle of wanting practical computer skills, job readiness, and career direction but feeling confused about where to start.

Visually communicate trust, guidance, and future confidence through a clean learning environment, approachable teachers, practical training setup, and motivated students.

The viewer should emotionally feel:
“I can trust this institute to guide me properly.”

## Scholarship / Offer Post
The design should create urgency and opportunity while making students feel:
“This is my chance to start without financial pressure.”

---

# 2. RESTAURANT

## Food Promotion Post
The design should psychologically trigger hunger, craving, and emotional satisfaction.

The viewer should instantly feel:
“I want to eat this right now.”

Use rich food textures, close-up presentation, warm lighting, freshness cues, and immersive food styling.

## Offer / Discount Post
The viewer should feel:
“This deal is too good to miss.”

## Family Dining Post
The viewer should feel:
“This is a place where I want to spend quality time with family and friends.”
